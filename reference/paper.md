# RiftWalker Academic Literature Foundation

**Overview:** This document outlines the primary academic literature that established the theoretical and mechanical foundation for the RiftWalker project. Each paper below played a specific, critical role in shaping our engineering decisions, from the unified propulsion architecture to the fluid-dynamic structural design.

---

### [1] Design and Performance Analysis of a Triphibious Robot with Tilting-Rotor Structure
* **Citation:** G. Zhong, J. Cao, X. Chai, and Y. Bai, "Design and Performance Analysis of a Triphibious Robot with Tilting-Rotor Structure," *IEEE Access*, vol. 9, pp. 10871–10879, 2021. 
* **DOI:** [10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3050182](https://doi.org)

#### How it influenced RiftWalker
This research validated tilt-rotor thrust vectoring across air, land, and water environments, directly inspiring our 90-degree servo-actuated motor tilt mechanism.
---

### [2] TriphiBot: A Triphibious Robot Combining FOC-based Propulsion with Eccentric Design
* **Citation:** X. Li, M. Lai, M. Zhang, J. Lin, T. Lai, J. Zhi, C. Xu, F. Gao, Y. Cao, "TriphiBot: A Triphibious Robot Combining FOC-based Propulsion with Eccentric Design," *arXiv preprint arXiv:2602.01385*, 2026.
* **Link:** [arXiv:2602.01385](https://arxiv.org)

#### How it influenced RiftWalker
This paper’s core thesis on unified propulsion architecture demonstrated unified cross-medium propulsion without extra actuators, confirming that reusing flight motors eliminates the parasitic mass of secondary drivetrains.
---

### [3] Design and Experiment of a Multimodal Aerial Aquatic Vehicle with Morphing Wing and Tilting Rotors
* **Citation:** M. Xia, B. Wang, and Z. Luo, "Design and Experiment of a Multimodal Aerial Aquatic Vehicle with Morphing Wing and Tilting Rotors," *Advances in Mechanical Engineering*, vol. 17, 2025.
* **DOI:** [10.1177/16878132251319142](https://doi.org)

#### How it influenced RiftWalker
This study evaluated structural and aerodynamic adaptations for cross-domain transitions, guiding our Z-shaped offset arm geometry to prevent propeller downwash from hitting the main chassis.

---

### [4] Design and Fabrication of a Multi-Terrain Triphibian Quadcopter for Airborne, Terrestrial, and Aquatic Mobility
* **Citation:** S. L. Pradeep Kumar, B. Deeban, D. Santhosh Kumar, and M. Sasi Kumar, "Design and Fabrication of a Multi-Terrain Triphibian Quadcopter for Airborne, Terrestrial, and Aquatic Mobility," *Discover Mechanical Engineering*, vol. 5, art. no. 20, 2026.
* **DOI:** [10.1007/s44245-025-00167-7](https://doi.org)

#### How it influenced RiftWalker
While other papers focused heavily on kinematics, this research provided a practical design and fabrication benchmark for multi-terrain quadcopters, shaping our modular chassis build and tactical real-world deployment cases.

---

### [5] CapsuleBot: A Novel Hybrid Aerial-Ground Bi-Copter Robot With Two Actuated-Wheel-Rotors
* **Citation:** Z. Zheng, Q. Cai, J. Wang, X. Xu, M. Cao, H. Yu, J. Li, J. Meng, and G. Lu, "CapsuleBot: A Novel Hybrid Aerial-Ground Bi-Copter Robot With Two Actuated-Wheel-Rotors," *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, vol. 10, no. 1, pp. 120–127, 2025.
* **DOI:** [10.1109/LRA.2024.3504232](https://doi.org)

#### How it influenced RiftWalker
This paper demonstrated dual-use actuator sharing between aerial flight and ground rolling, inspiring our reliance on differential thrust and dual-purpose wheels over heavy steering racks.
